# Manuscript capture attribution

The ten `preprint-viewer-page*.png` captures reproduce the data-descriptor preprint
by Selina Uhlendorff, Ruwen Fulek, Jonas Eimler, Miriam Pein-Hackelbusch and
Björn Frahm (2026), *Data Descriptor: Multimodal Dataset of Bioreactor Cell
Cultivations Including Turbidity, Permittivity, O₂ and CO₂ Measurements*, version 1.

Source: https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202611.0377.v1

© 2026 the authors. Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The captures show the original manuscript rendered in a browser with its controls
and page framing. Scientific content was not edited. They are JPEG payloads with
`.png` filenames, not the original PDF. Viewer page 02 is manuscript page 1;
viewer page 11 is manuscript page 10. Exact captured bytes are recorded in
`SHA256SUMS.txt`.

[Full paper and dataset citations](../../../CITATIONS.md) are maintained separately.
The preprint license is not inferred to license the separate Zenodo dataset.
