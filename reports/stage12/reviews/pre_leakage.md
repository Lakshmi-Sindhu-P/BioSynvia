# Preimplementation temporal and experiment-group leakage review

Reviewer: fresh-context native Goldfish substitute; 2026-11-05. Scope: executable specification, existing preparation implementation and bounded inspection of v3 metadata/lineage. No fitting, new broad EDA or source changes. Applied the karpathy-guidelines review skill. This is an engineering review, not scientific approval.

## Prioritized actionable findings

1. **P1 — Strict status rules make the stated eight-core coverage criterion degenerate.** Evidence: `docs/stage12-executable-spec.md:32–37` excludes absent/unknown Status, while `biosynvia/preparation.py:72,95–96` accepts absent Status by skipping the rejection. A bounded count of v3 `feature_lineage.csv` found each of pH, Dissolved O2 and Temperature has 378 `not_provided` / `usable_for_inspection` entries; all three belong to `CORE_FIELDS`. Therefore recomputed strict eight-core complete-row coverage is zero in every reactor, and “highest minimum reactor core coverage” (`spec:54–59`) cannot distinguish groups. Recommendation: recompute eligibility from raw evidence rather than inherit `inspection_value`; freeze the precise core/coverage rule, detect and report all-zero coverage, and show the resulting lexicographic tie selection explicitly. Do not silently approve unknown Status or change core fields to manufacture support. Keep scientific/protocol approval false; any alternative status/core scenario must be separately named and approved before modeling.

2. **P1 — Define a target-independent coverage denominator and freeze one split across scenarios.** Evidence: existing `inspection_candidate` is `~target_review_required & core_features_complete` (`preparation.py:231–235`), and `target_review_required` depends on VCD values (`:126–129`). Its 333 rows are unsuitable for target-blind group selection. The spec names input coverage but not its exact numerator, denominator, governing age/availability scenario or behavior for unavailable core fields. Recommendation: use all preserved assay acquisition cutoffs within each reactor as the fixed denominator, independent of positive/nonnegative target masks; count strict core-complete inputs in the numerator, take the minimum of the two reactor fractions, then apply the specified mode/ID tie rule. Declare the governing scenario before selection and retain the same group IDs for 1/5/15-minute, target-mask and feature-family sensitivities. Verify split invariance after replacing, permuting and nulling all VCD/SEM values and target-review flags. Store reactor counts/fractions and group scores alongside IDs.

3. **P1 — Make acquisition and availability predicates executable, including equality and delayed records.** Evidence: `snapshot_stream` uses only acquisition time with `searchsorted(..., side="right")` (`preparation.py:80–86`); v3 manifest states result availability is unknown. `spec:28–31,50–52` correctly requires separate retrospective and strict scenarios, but the exact predicate and unknown-field representation remain unspecified. Recommendation: preserve unknown availability as null with evidence status, never substitute acquisition time into the strict field. A strict candidate must be from the same reactor with known acquisition and sensor-availability times no later than the assay acquisition cutoff; the assumed-acquisition scenario may explicitly substitute the documented assumption. State the treatment of exact-cutoff timestamps and clock precision/order uncertainty. Test a sensor acquired before cutoff but available after it, unknown availability, a record acquired after cutoff, and foreign-reactor values. Target assay result availability must not move the inference cutoff later. If every strict candidate is blocked, report zero coverage/abstention rather than a deployable score.

4. **P1 — Put every learned choice inside the appropriate grouped training partition.** Evidence: `spec:62–71` lists the correct operations and inner grouped tuning, but “development training groups” can be misread as all development groups before leave-one-experiment-out scoring. Recommendation: document an outer development fold holding out an entire EXP ID (both reactors), an inner EXP-grouped search on only that outer fold's training groups, and refitting the selected pipeline on that outer training partition before scoring its held-out experiment. Include feature/window/freshness selection, component counts, family choice and any learned inactive-channel thresholds in that boundary. Fix assay masks/scenarios in advance. Following the frozen development selection, refit on all development groups and evaluate the two final groups once; no final-test-based family, mask, scenario or hyperparameter choice. Store explicit outer/inner group memberships and assert pair/disjointness invariants.

5. **P2 — Preserve prefix-only quality decisions and test mutation of future raw records, not just future snapshots.** Evidence: existing fitted-zero history uses prefix `cummin` (`preparation.py:76–78,100–101`), which is temporally safer than whole-run uniqueness; however quality is represented by one precedence-ordered `reason` (`:88–101`) and the v3 whole-stream descriptive artifacts include future data. Recommendation: compute independent flags exclusively from information available at each scenario's cutoff, preserve the raw status and competing duplicates, and explicitly define how missing/nonfinite prefix values affect an “all observed past values zero” rule. Do not use whole-run `nunique`, end time, future activation, global fitted thresholds or later status to change an earlier feature. A future-mutation test should append/change future raw values/status/timestamps and confirm earlier values, independent quality flags and eligibility stay unchanged; test availability-delayed backfilled records separately. Coverage summaries may use the full supplied history for split design, but must never become per-prediction inputs.

6. **P2 — Record the prior target exposure and constrain the final evaluation claim.** Evidence: preparation explicitly calls `correlations(processed, stage)` before any split (`preparation.py:253`); `:278–294` computes pooled feature-target, positive-only and within-run-centered associations across all groups. `reports/preparation/report.md` also reports selected associations and plots. The spec correctly discloses this at `:60–61`; the later holdout cannot erase that exposure. Recommendation: list these artifacts as prior exposure in the frozen protocol and manifest, prohibit re-running or consulting final-group target associations to revise choices, and describe final results as a prospectively reserved internal evaluation after all-group exploration. Disclose the supported-input selection bias and two-test-group limitation beside results. Independent future experiments are required for an untouched external-validation claim; group bootstrap cannot restore this property.

## Overall disposition

This is a reasonable engineering proposal if the revised split contract is frozen explicitly, preserves the original-eight result, and labels the favorable-input selection limitation. Compare coverage fractions exactly (counts/rational cross-products), then sort tied EXP IDs lexicographically; avoid rounding scores before selecting. Use the same resulting group IDs for every later scenario, including scenarios with zero support.

The specification has the right leakage boundaries and explicitly forbids fitting. Proceed with protocol/data engineering after resolving findings 1–3 as explicit contracts and carrying findings 4–6 into the benchmark protocol and verification. Existing v3 eligibility and `inspection_subset` must remain inspection evidence, never an approved split/training source. No scientific approval or model-fit permission is granted by this review.
