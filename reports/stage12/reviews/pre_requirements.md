## Findings

1. **P2 — Make the target-decision schema contain the complete required target record.** Requirements: **FR-05, SR-DAT-007, G1**. Stage 1 item 1 names the recommendation, zero sensitivities and approval flags, but does not explicitly require source column, documented unit, assay meaning, sampling cadence and valid counts per experiment in the target decision itself. These are required even when approval remains false; leaving them scattered across reports makes the eventual approval object ambiguous. **Recommendation:** require these fields, evidence references and explicit unknown statuses in `target_decision`; report proposed positive/nonnegative counts and actual assay cadence per experiment, separately from scientific acceptance. Do not infer assay meaning from the VCD label alone or mark G1 passed.

2. **P2 — Fix the benchmark ranking rule and required comparison outputs before later evaluation.** Requirements: **FR-09/FR-10, SR-MOD-005, SR-MOD-006, SR-DT-008**. Stage 2 item 3 proposes MAE/RMSE/bias and equal-weight experiment summaries but does not explicitly identify experiment-macro MAE as the primary ranking statistic, or require baseline deltas and experiment spread. The retained SRD specifies these; treating all proposed metrics as co-primary leaves later champion selection discretionary. **Recommendation:** encode primary ranking as macro-average experiment MAE, with RMSE/bias secondary; specify per-experiment baseline deltas, spread, failures and abstentions. Freeze this protocol without calculating any model performance now.

3. **P2 — Add an explicit raw-to-aligned time-policy record rather than relying on issue notes.** Requirements: **FR-04, SR-DAT-005, SR-DT-003**. The spec appropriately calls for raw past-only selection and acquisition/availability fields, but the retained requirement also asks for each file's original-to-aligned timestamp transformation or sampling policy and flags against a reviewed tolerance. The EXP011 issue alone is not that systematic record. **Recommendation:** record source time origin/units, observed raw/aligned transformation or unresolved status, any offset/resampling evidence and mismatch flags per stream/file. Where no approved tolerance exists, retain descriptive differences and an unapproved tolerance status rather than silently accepting alignment. Raw-only feature construction can remain the proposed path.

4. **P2 — Bound the completion claim with explicit requirement/gate dispositions.** Requirements: **G1/G2/G3, M1/M2, DT-06, SR-DT-012, CG1; SRD v0.2 §7 requirement-status rules**. Scientific approval is correctly false, but the spec asks for a completed Stage 1/2 packet without a requirement-level statement that this does not complete data-ready/model-ready or CG1. Full DT-06 characterization, growth-window protocols and the relevant scientific acceptances are outside this bounded work. **Recommendation:** include a small traceability/status table identifying implemented/tested engineering subsets and blocked/deferred scientific or later-stage requirements. Keep G1 and model readiness open, distinguish provisional artifact validation from scientific release approval, and explicitly defer full characterization/component acceptance. Do not expand this task into fitting, growth analysis or a Track B release to close those rows.

## Disposition

Proceed with bounded protocol engineering after the above clarifications; none requires new scientific assumptions or model fitting. Keep owner protocol approval, independent scientific evidence and engineering validation as separate records. This review is not a scientific sign-off or a declaration that any product gate has passed.
