# Research paper and dataset citations

BioSynvia uses the following two distinct research outputs. Cite the dataset for
the observations and the data-descriptor preprint for the experimental methods and
interpretation. The original authors produced the study and dataset; BioSynvia's
contribution is the independent software, auditing and analysis documented here.
